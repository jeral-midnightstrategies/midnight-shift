Professional telemarketing

Real conversations, made on your behalf

Not a boiler room. Not a robocall farm. Trained callers who understand the Singapore property market, speaking to your leads the way you would — promptly, politely and persistently.

Why calls still matter

Speed and persistence win conversations

A property lead is a person mid-decision. The agent who reaches them first, listens properly and follows up reliably is the agent they remember. The problem is that doing this well is a full-time job — and you already have one.

Midnight Shift takes over the part that eats your day: the dialing, the redialing, the "call me back next week", the logging. You keep the part that needs you: the meeting.

See where calling fits in the system
  • Prompt first contact — new leads are prioritised while interest is fresh.
  • Structured retry cycles — no-answers are attempted again at varied times, not written off.
  • Sensible calling hours — outreach at respectful times that suit working professionals.
  • Every outcome logged — connects, callbacks, objections and next steps recorded in the CRM.
  • Message follow-ups — where appropriate, calls are paired with a written message so leads can respond on their terms.
