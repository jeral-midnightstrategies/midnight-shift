Common questions

Before you ask…

Will leads know the call is outsourced?

Calls are made on behalf of your business, using a script you approve and information you provide. Callers introduce themselves as calling from your team, and every qualified conversation is handed to you with full context — so the transition feels seamless to the prospect.

Do I need to already have leads?

The system works best if you have an existing flow of leads or a database of past enquiries. If you also need lead generation, our parent agency Midnight Strategies SG runs Meta and Google campaigns for property agents — the two plug together naturally.

Is this compliant with Singapore's DNC rules?

Compliance is built into the workflow: numbers are screened against DNC registry requirements where required, consent context is recorded per lead, and calling practices follow PDPA guidelines. See our data handling page for details.
