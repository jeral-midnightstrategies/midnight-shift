How it works

A disciplined process, not ad-hoc calling

Midnight Shift runs your lead follow-up as a managed operation. Here is the full workflow — from the moment a lead enters your world to the moment you shake their hand at an appointment.

  1. Connect your lead sources

    We consolidate leads from wherever they already come from — no need to change how you generate them.

    • Meta Ads and Google Ads campaigns (new leads flow in automatically)
    • Property portal enquiries
    • New launch, resale and seller campaign lists
    • Roadshow sign-ups, referrals and past enquiries
    • Old CRM databases and spreadsheets — cleaned, de-duplicated and imported

  2. Set up your CRM & approve the script

    Before a single call is made, we set the foundation together:

    • Your leads organised in one CRM with sources, tags and history
    • A calling script written for your campaign type — and approved by you
    • Qualification criteria agreed: what counts as a serious prospect for you
    • Your availability and preferred appointment slots captured
    • DNC screening and consent checks applied to the calling list where required

  3. Structured calling cycles begin

    Trained, property-fluent callers work your list in systematic cycles:

    • New leads are prioritised for prompt first contact
    • No-answers are retried at varied, sensible times — not abandoned after one attempt
    • Every attempt, connect and outcome is logged in the CRM as it happens
    • Polite, professional conversations under your brand — never pressure tactics

  4. Every lead gets qualified & categorised

    Conversations follow your qualification criteria, so each lead lands in a clear category:

    • Qualified — genuine intent, ready to speak with you
    • Nurture — real interest, but the timeline is later; scheduled for a future touchpoint
    • Not interested — closed off politely, so your list stays clean
    • Invalid — wrong numbers and junk filtered out of your pipeline

    Timeline, budget signals, property context and objections are captured in the notes — not just a status.

  5. Appointments land in your calendar

    When a lead is qualified and willing to meet:

    • The appointment is coordinated around your availability
    • You receive a full context brief — who they are, what they want, what was said
    • Confirmations and reminders reduce no-shows
    • If a lead reschedules or goes quiet, we chase the rebooking — not you

  6. Follow-up & reporting continue in the background

    The system keeps working between appointments:

    • Nurture leads get scheduled callbacks when their timeline approaches
    • Your dashboard shows call activity, outcomes and pipeline movement
    • Scripts and targeting are refined based on what conversations reveal
    • Your database stays clean, current and yours — exportable any time
