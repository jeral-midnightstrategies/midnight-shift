What lives in the CRM

Built for how property leads actually behave

Source tracking

Every lead tagged with where it came from — Meta, Google, portal, roadshow, referral — so you know which channels produce serious prospects.

Status & stage

New, contacted, qualified, nurture, appointment, not interested, invalid — a clean pipeline instead of a mystery list.

Full conversation timeline

Calls, WhatsApp touchpoints, notes and callbacks in chronological order. Pick up any lead's story in seconds.

Qualification details

Timeline, intent, property type, buyer/seller context and objections — captured as structured fields, not vague memory.

Team assignment

For teams: leads assigned to the right member with visibility for the leader — activity, outcomes and appointments per person.

Export any time

Your database remains yours. Download your full lead list with notes and statuses whenever you want — no lock-in, no ransom.

For team leaders

Visibility without micromanaging

Team leaders see the whole operation from one dashboard: lead activity, call outcomes, appointment flow and follow-up discipline per member — without chasing anyone for updates.

  • Which campaigns are producing conversations
  • Where leads are getting stuck in the pipeline
  • Who has appointments coming up, and from which sources
  • Whether nurture leads are actually being re-contacted
